## Backup Talking Points (if questions)

### "How is this different from X marketplace?"
"Three things: continuous verification (not just at launch), on-chain proof (real BSC transactions), and anti-manipulation (Sybil detection + verified reviews)."

### "What if an agent is malicious?"
"Four layers: granular permissions, spending limits, session expiration, and emergency revocation. You're always in control."

### "How do you prevent fake reviews?"
"Two ways: only users who hired can review (verified reviews), and Sybil detection catches coordinated fake accounts."

### "What about gas costs?"
"We use a relayer wallet for health checks, so users don't pay gas. For hiring, it's a one-time session creation on BSC."

### "Can this scale to 200,000 agents?"
"Yes. Our architecture is designed for it: 40 scalable API endpoints, Redis caching ready, background job queue ready, read replicas supported."

### "What's the business model?"
"Five pricing models supported: FREE (discovery), PER_TASK, SUBSCRIPTION, USAGE_BASED, PERFORMANCE_BASED. Developers choose, marketplace takes a small fee."
